Adventures of Pip

Creative Director · Lead Game Designer

A platform adventure built around transforming between one-pixel, 8-bit, and 32-bit forms.

PROJECT

Adventures of Pip

ROLE

Creative Director · Lead Game Designer

PLATFORM

Console · PC · Mobile · 2015

01 / DESIGN CHALLENGE

Turn visual resolution into a meaningful gameplay system rather than a cosmetic gimmick, while ensuring every form remains useful throughout the game.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

  • Created the transformation pillar and the strengths and weaknesses of each form.
  • Established the creative vision, world, characters, tone, and visual progression.
  • Directed level and puzzle design around switching forms.
  • Led the project from concept through crowdfunding, production, and multi-platform release.

03 / DESIGN DETAILS

Three forms

The one-pixel form is light and agile, the 8-bit form supports nimble platforming, and the 32-bit form provides strength and combat capability.

Meaningful tradeoffs

No form is a simple upgrade. Movement, combat, weight, and environmental interactions create reasons to transform in both directions.

Unified theme

The visual-resolution premise informs mechanics, story, environments, enemies, and the identity of the hero.

04 / OUTCOME

Adventures of Pip shipped across Wii U, PlayStation, Xbox, PC, and mobile platforms after a successful Kickstarter campaign.
