About

20+Years as a Creative Director or Designer in a lead role
46+Shipped titles across mobile and console

I’m a Lead Game Designer and Creative Director with experience guiding games from early vision through launch and live updates. I specialize in building strong gameplay and meta systems, shaping narrative-driven experiences, and aligning multidisciplinary teams around a clear creative direction.

I’m particularly interested in where player psychology, interactivity, and systems design meet—and how thoughtful design choices can improve engagement, retention, and long-term player satisfaction. I enjoy projects that push design boundaries while keeping creative vision and practical execution closely aligned.
