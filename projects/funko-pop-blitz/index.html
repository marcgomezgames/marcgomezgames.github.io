A collectible match game designed around recognizable characters, short sessions, and long-term collection goals.

PROJECT

Funko Pop! Blitz

ROLE

Designer · Creative Director

PLATFORM

iOS · Android · 2019

01 / DESIGN CHALLENGE

Support the appeal of collecting licensed Funko characters with a progression structure that gives players clear reasons to return beyond individual puzzle sessions.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

  • Designed core and meta systems for Tic Toc Games’ first Blitz-style mobile title.
  • Proposed a progression-road structure for long-term goals.
  • Connected character collection with gameplay value and collectible synergies.
  • Directed the overall product vision during soft launch development.

03 / DESIGN DETAILS

Progression road

A visible route gives players an understandable sense of forward movement, upcoming rewards, and session goals.

Collection value

Characters are more than visual rewards; their synergies and gameplay uses support experimentation and roster growth.

Licensed content

The structure accommodates a broad range of recognizable properties while retaining a consistent game framework.

04 / OUTCOME

The project established a scalable design foundation for a licensed, collection-driven mobile puzzle game.
