A bubble puzzle game whose progression centers on restoring and expanding the Smurfs’ village.

PROJECT

Smurfs Bubble Story

ROLE

Designer · Creative Director

PLATFORM

iOS · Android · 2017

01 / DESIGN CHALLENGE

Replace the familiar linear saga map with progression that feels native to the Smurfs world and gives puzzle completion a visible purpose.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

  • Led the full game design and creative direction.
  • Created the village-building meta progression.
  • Connected level completion, rewards, and restoration goals.
  • Directed tone and presentation around the licensed property.

03 / DESIGN DETAILS

Village meta

Progress is represented by rebuilding a familiar place rather than moving a marker along a path.

Visible rewards

Completed levels contribute to changes players can see in the village, making advancement concrete and personal.

Brand alignment

The cooperative, restorative premise supports the tone and social world associated with the Smurfs.

04 / OUTCOME

The shipped game differentiated its long-term progression from conventional bubble-puzzle competitors through a property-specific village structure.
