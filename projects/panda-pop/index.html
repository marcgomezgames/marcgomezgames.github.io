Panda Pop

Game Design · Creative Direction

A long-running bubble puzzle game in which power-ups, level goals, and progression remain immediately understandable on a small screen.

PROJECT

Panda Pop

ROLE

Game Design · Creative Direction

PLATFORM

iOS · Android · 2013

01 / DESIGN CHALLENGE

Create a power-up system that adds strategic choice and visual excitement without interrupting the simple aim-and-shoot loop or overwhelming casual players.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

  • Designed the lantern power-up system and its relationship to colored matches.
  • Defined clear activation rules and readable feedback for mobile play.
  • Balanced immediate usefulness with longer-term strategic value.
  • Worked within an established live puzzle framework designed for frequent content updates.

03 / DESIGN DETAILS

Lantern system

Colored matches charge corresponding lanterns. This connects the power-up layer directly to actions the player already understands instead of adding a separate resource system.

Choice and timing

Players decide when a charged ability creates the most value, introducing planning without slowing the core bubble-shooting rhythm.

Mobile clarity

Color, placement, and feedback make charge state and activation results readable at phone scale.

04 / OUTCOME

Panda Pop surpassed 100 million downloads and reached the top five puzzle games in more than 93 countries.
