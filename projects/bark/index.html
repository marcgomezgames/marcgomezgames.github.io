B.ARK

Creative Director · Lead Game Designer · Art Director

An approachable four-player cooperative shoot-’em-up created around rescue, teamwork, and readable shared-screen play.

PROJECT

B.ARK

ROLE

Creative Director · Lead Game Designer · Art Director

PLATFORM

Nintendo Switch · PC · 2021

01 / DESIGN CHALLENGE

Traditional shoot-’em-ups can be visually overwhelming and difficult for new players to enter. The design needed to preserve the energy of the genre while welcoming families and mixed-skill groups.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

  • Created the original game concept and cooperative design pillars.
  • Directed game design, visual style, tone, characters, and presentation.
  • Designed rescue interactions and combination attacks that reward cooperation.
  • Used narrative context and clear character roles to make a traditionally hardcore genre easier to understand.

03 / DESIGN DETAILS

Cooperative structure

Four players share the screen and support one another through rescues, coordinated positioning, and combination attacks. Team success matters more than individual score chasing.

Readable roles

Distinct characters, silhouettes, colors, and abilities help players understand their place in the group quickly—even when the screen becomes busy.

Accessible tone

The animal cast and playful presentation broaden the audience without removing the mechanical depth expected from the genre.

04 / OUTCOME

B.ARK shipped on Nintendo Switch and PC and was featured in Nintendo’s Indie World Showcase.
