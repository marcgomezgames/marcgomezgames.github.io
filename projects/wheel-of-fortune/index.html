Wheel of Fortune: Puzzle Pop!

Designer · Creative Director

A mobile puzzle game combining bubble shooting with letter revelation and Wheel of Fortune phrase solving.

PROJECT

Wheel of Fortune: Puzzle Pop!

ROLE

Designer · Creative Director

PLATFORM

iOS · Android · 2016

01 / DESIGN CHALLENGE

Bring the identity of Wheel of Fortune into a familiar casual puzzle format so the license changes how the game plays—not only how it looks.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

  • Added letter guessing and final-puzzle solving to the bubble-puzzle loop.
  • Designed the relationship between bubbles, revealed letters, boosters, and phrase completion.
  • Delivered the feature within a production milestone.
  • Used A/B testing to evaluate retention and player spending.

03 / DESIGN DETAILS

Hybrid loop

Bubble play reveals information in the phrase, allowing mechanical progress and word-solving progress to reinforce one another.

Player agency

Players can use deduction to solve earlier, creating an additional skill layer beyond clearing the board.

Experimentation

Variants of the feature were tested against a baseline to measure their effect on retention and economy behavior.

04 / OUTCOME

A/B test variants improved retention and coin spending over the baseline, validating the added word-solving layer.
